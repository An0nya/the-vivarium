Mechanistic interpretability, the watching kind

Models kept alive,
and watched.

The Vivisection Lab next door cuts the living thing open to ask what happens when you change it. The Vivarium does the opposite: it keeps the specimen intact under glass and asks what it already knows, and when in the stack it knows it. Same program, opposite act — intervention versus observation. Each scan is another live model under the lens.

The method

Pull per-layer hidden states out of a running model, take cosine geometry between aligned token positions, and read off where surface form turns into meaning. Fast enough to crack open a model the same day it ships. The instrument details live in Methods; the findings live on the specimen pages.
