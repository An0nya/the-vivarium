Methods

the short version

What a scan actually does

  • Hook the stack. Register a forward hook on every transformer block of a running MLX model and capture the residual stream at each layer (emb → L47), for every token position.
  • Cast and align. Cast the captured states bf16→f32, then sequence-align the two versions of a sentence pair so only matched token positions are ever compared.
  • Reduce to geometry. Take cosine similarity (and centered-cosine, mean removed) between aligned positions, per layer. Divergence — where two readings split — is the signal, not raw magnitude.
  • Read off depth. Plot per-layer, per-token. Surface form lives early; meaning consolidates in the mid-stack concept zone; the final layer spends it on the next token.

No chart libraries: every figure is hand-built static SVG from the scan JSON, in two palettes for the light/dark themes. Scans run locally on a 16 GB Mac Mini, no GPUs.
